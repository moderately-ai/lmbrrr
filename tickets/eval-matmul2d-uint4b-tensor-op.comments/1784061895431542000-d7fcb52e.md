LEAD (from the MSL 4.1 spec read during the packed_numeric eval, 2026-07-14): matmul2d TensorOps (MSL spec 7.2.1, Table 7.3) support `bfloat x uint4b_format -> float` and `bfloat x uint4b_format -> bfloat` IN HARDWARE on "Metal 4 and OS 26.4"+ — i.e. the M3 referee (macOS 27) can run a matmul whose B operand is packed 4-bit unsigned against bf16 activations on the matrix units, no dequant kernel.

WHY THIS IS THE m>=2 LANE, NOT m=1: the m=1 GEMV is bandwidth/issue-bound and the hand-tuned masked-FMA kernel already sits at 88% of roof (nr2sg2 ship); packed_numeric unpack LOST there (see m3-macos26-eval-suite verdict — 0.90x). Tensor ops need tile shapes (M-dim amortization) to pay; the candidates are the wide m in [2,8] verify/spec kernels (current wide-v2 dequant-once kernel: lm_head m=2 at 1.44-1.53x vs mv) and prefill.

OPEN QUESTIONS THE EVAL MUST ANSWER (in order, cheapest first):
1. Scale plumbing: q4_K has per-32-value sub-block scales + per-block d/dmin. matmul2d's uint4b B operand is raw packed nibbles with NO scale concept — the kernel must loop per 256-value block, run the tensor op on the raw nibbles, and fold scales into the accumulator between ops (C += d_b * (A x Q_b) - dmin_b * rowsum term). Is that expressible with cooperative_tensor accumulation at acceptable overhead? Spec sections 7.2.1 (matmul2d run/descriptor) + 2.22 (tensor types) are the reading list.
2. Shape floor: what is the minimum M x N x K tile where the matmul unit beats the wide-v2 kernel? Our verify m is 2-8 — possibly below the unit's efficiency floor. Micro-bench: lm_head (248094x1024) and mlp shapes at m in {2,4,8,16,32,128}.
3. Toolchain gate: TensorOps compile under -std=metal4.0 (offline toolchain OK per Table 7.3 "Metal 4" rows for half; the uint4b rows say "Metal 4 and OS 26.4" — verify whether the RUNTIME 4.1 trick from m3-macos26-eval-suite is needed or plain 4.0 suffices on macOS 27).
4. If the micro wins at m in [2,8]: production port behind LMBRRR_Q4K_WIDE variant routing, margin-class gates (drafter golden re-bless allowed), M3 A/B per the measurement protocol.

PRIOR ART TO CHECK FIRST: llama.cpp and MLX main branches for any matmul2d/tensor-op adoption (they had none as of the 2026-07-13 A2A read); Apple's metal-cpp samples for a worked q4 example.

DISPOSITION: exploratory, m>=2 lane only, does not touch the shipped decode path. Parked behind the spec-lane unpark conditions (verify economics) OR prefill work — whichever revives first. Do NOT start unsolicited while the spec lane is parked.
