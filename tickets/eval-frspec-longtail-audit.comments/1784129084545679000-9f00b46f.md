AUDIT EXECUTED (2026-07-15) — the core question (does the 32k FR-Spec draft slice damage classes by dropping tokens the target keeps?) is now answered per-class, with a causal in-loop A/B. Method: deployed-q4k target greedy over the 6 suite classes (3 questions each, 128 tok), measured the fraction of the target's own generated tokens falling outside the top-N frspec slice; then ran 32k vs 64k vs full-vocab draft in-loop.

OUT-OF-SLICE FRACTION (per class, target's greedy tokens):
- summarization: 7.8% out-of-32k, 1.3% out-of-64k
- qa:            5.4% out-of-32k, 0.0% out-of-64k
- math, coding, translation, writing: ~0% at both (0.0-0.5%)
The dropped tokens are genuine rare content: peacock->"pe|acock", "surrogate", "crews", and non-English "orang"/"Kecil" (Indonesian) in a summ prompt. So the damage is REAL and concentrated in the content-heavy classes (qa/summ), NOT in translation (translation is 0% oos — its weakness is variance, not the slice).

CAUSAL A/B (artifacts/suite-slicefull-*, tag slicefull):
- 64k RECOVERS tau on the gap questions (qa q324 1.69->1.98 with tok/s 175->190; summ q244 2.05->2.19) — mechanism confirmed.
- BUT full 6-class 32k->64k = MEAN -1.9%, FAILS the ship bar: the 4 no-gap classes each regress ~3% (pure draft-compute tax of the 2x sliced-head read; verify unchanged, tau identical). Only qa/summ gain.
- tau-vs-vocab is NON-MONOTONIC: full-vocab tau is LOWER than 64k everywhere (drafter self-confusion over more vocab). summ q246 already dips at 64k (2.27->2.23).

VERDICT: global slice widening is a per-class trade that nets NEGATIVE (tax on 4, gain on 2). The 32k slice is well-tuned globally. The real lever is a TARGETED/ADAPTIVE slice for content-heavy classes (add the specific rare content tokens — a handful, ~7 qa / ~30 summ across 3 questions — without doubling N; a frequency ranking can't surface prompt-specific rare content, so this needs a coverage-aware ranking or a task-conditioned slice). VALUE GROWS WITH DRAFTER QUALITY (see spec-loop-economics-recovery 786969b9): coverage is marginal while the drafter mostly guesses wrong; it becomes binding once the drafter is accurate and its residual misses are "right-but-out-of-slice". Recommend: keep 32k now; revisit adaptive slice after the drafter improves.
