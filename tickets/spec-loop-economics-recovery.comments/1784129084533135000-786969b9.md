WHAT THE EVIDENCE ACTUALLY SHOWS (certain, causal):
- **The drafter's own argmax quality is the limiter — training HAS headroom.** Proof: FULL-vocab draft gives LOWER tau than sliced (writing/math tau identical 32k vs full; qa/summ tau LOWER at full than 64k). With 100% coverage the drafter should accept more; it accepts less, because more vocab lets the imperfect head argmax to rare wrong tokens (self-confusion). The head, not the vocab or the target entropy, is the bottleneck.
