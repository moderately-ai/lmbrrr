CLOSING on arithmetic (2026-07-12): state traffic is 18 layers x 2 MB read+write = 36 MB/token, ~0.1 ms at the measured ~350 GB/s (~2% of a 4.65 ms token) - and the v2 chunk kernels already keep the state register-resident across the l-loop, so the addressable traffic is decode-only and halving it buys <0.05 ms/token. Against that, the ollama bf16-state precedent (quality corruption from compounding rounding in the recurrence) is a real risk the fused kernels deliberately avoided with f32 state. Bounded upside, known hazard: not worth the change.
