mm2d CEILING PROVEN BY M-INVARIANCE (2026-07-17, gpudebug intervention, m=5 vs m=8, M3 Pro): the low f32 utilization (~40%) that caps the instruction-bound mm2d is INVARIANT to M — m=8 (full 8-row tile) vs m=5 (62.5% filled) give instruction_util 22.86 vs 23.31%, f32_util 40.94 vs 40.10%, BW 55.2 vs 54.3 GB/s, occ 51 vs 48% — IDENTICAL. This REFUTES the small-M-tile-underfill hypothesis: filling the tile does nothing. The 40% f32 cap is structural to the matmul2d cooperative-tensor PRIMITIVE + the 40-tile BK=128 K-loop, not the M-fill. It also localizes the cap to op.run (M-invariant) NOT the fold (a small M-invariant epilogue op.run overlaps), so a fold accumulator-split ILP cannot move it (refuted by mechanism). So mm2d verify is at its M3-LOCAL CEILING, proven by FIVE refuted interventions: (1) M-underfill [m-invariant], (2) t32 tile/higher-occupancy [+2.6ms in-loop 2026-07-14], (3) bigger M-tiles [negative], (4) index-hoist [+0.08%], (5) fullk fold-removal [needs the pow2 requant = +18.2% weight error, refuted M3-local]. The ONLY surviving lever is the gated Modal fullk-from-original requant (uncertain PPL). The 40% primitive utilization at 54 GB/s IS the matmul2d op's practical ceiling for the Q2_0 verify shape on M3 — the wall is the primitive's issue efficiency on ternary-unpacked operands, an architectural (pre-M5) limit.

ENGINE-WIDE BOTTLENECK CLASS — CAPSTONE (2026-07-17, gpudebug on every isolated hot kernel, M3 Pro, pinned-high): the M3 Bonsai engine is INSTRUCTION-ISSUE-BOUND across its hot kernels, NOT memory-bandwidth-bound. mm2d verify (m=5): instruction_throughput_limiter 70.95% at 54 GB/s (36% of the 150 GB/s peak). v2_decode (deltanet decode, m=1): 70.62% at 52 GB/s (35%). decode-mv (m=1): 58.78% at 111 GB/s (74% — the one closest to the BW roof). DeltaNet chunk (verify recurrence): was latency/occupancy-bound at 8% occ (FIXED by F1). So three of four hot kernels sit at ~35-54% of peak DRAM BW with the ISSUE PORT (2-bit unpack + recurrence/MAC arithmetic + planar addressing) as the wall — proven by: BW counters far below peak + instruction_throughput the dominant limiter + f32/integer the top pipes + interventions that removed instructions (F1 occupancy) MOVING it while ones that only touched bandwidth/addressing (mm2d index-hoist) DID NOT. CONSEQUENCE for strategy: bandwidth-spending tricks (wider weight codes, byte-aligned unpack) are DEAD (BW is not the wall — confirmed by B4 wider-unpack refutation and the mv being instruction-limited); the ONLY software lever class that pays is FEWER ISSUED INSTRUCTIONS per weight/element (mostly exhausted: select-form map optimal, index-hoist refuted, fold needs a refuted pow2 requant) — which is why the frontier is now dominated by the ACCEPTANCE axis (gated drafter retrains, fewer verify rounds) and the M5 matrix unit (D1 — offloads the MAC issue to a tensor unit, the hardware answer to an issue-bound engine). This is the mechanistic why behind "verify near its practical M3 ceiling".

VERIFY-WALL WHITE-BOX (2026-07-17, gpudebug counters on the isolated mm2d_q2_0 verify kernel, m=5, M3 Pro — the mandated bottleneck-class capture for the 84%-of-round kernel): BOTTLENECK CLASS NAMED + PROVEN = **instruction-issue-bound** (instruction_throughput_limiter 70.95%, even higher than the decode-mv's 58.78%) at **54.32 GB/s (41.5 read + 12.8 write, ~36% of the 150 GB/s M3 Pro peak — NOT bandwidth-bound, large BW headroom)**; occupancy healthy (47.89%), alu 42%. Instruction-class split: **f32_limiter 50.21% but only 40% util (the FMA is STALLED, not saturated)**; **address_generation 36.93% limiter at just 12.59% util (serializing badly)**; integer_and_complex 35.55% + integer_and_conditional 30.13% (the 2-bit unpack); f16/control_flow negligible. MECHANISM found in source: the K-loop fold called `p.get_multidimensional_index(i)` EVERY K-tile (NJ=40) though the cooperative-tensor index->(n,m) map is tile-invariant — p is a fresh tensor each tile so no CSE — recomputing the same (n,m) 40x = the redundant address-gen starving the FMA. INTERVENTION + REFUTATION (byte-exact, non-requant): hoisted the index out of the K-loop, precomputed (n,m) once into registers — 5-round rotated spec A/B (planar) = byte-parity IDENTICAL but **+0.08% tok/s / -0.04% verify = ZERO**. REVERTED (candle cd2499cc). The address_generation 37%-limiter was a CANDIDATE the intervention DID NOT MOVE — this is the goal's exact lesson (a counter names a candidate; the intervention decides). Attribution: either the compiler already CSE'd get_multidimensional_index (a pure fn of lane+compile-time descriptor), or the address-gen is the per-tile d_p SCALE-LOADS (NJ*cap device loads), not the index compute — the hoist reduced the index arithmetic but not the loads. Cross-check: the strip-probe (probe_fullk = NO K-loop -15-18%) shows the removable K-loop cost is real but lives in the FOLD/scale-load/cooperative-tensor-realloc, which the cheap hoist does NOT touch. So the CHEAP mm2d lever is CLOSED; the fullk/power-of-two-requant restructure [mm2d-fullk-pow2-requant-verify] was the candidate deeper lever but its M3-LOCAL path is now REFUTED-WITH-MECHANISM (2026-07-17, `gguf pow2-scales`): requantizing the deployed Q2_0 weights to per-32 is EXACT under arbitrary scales (granularity is free) but +18.2% weight error under the pow2 constraint (the Q2_0 per-128 scales sit median 0.180 log2 from a pow2) -> garbage. Per-32 cannot help because the input is already per-128-quantized. The only surviving fullk path is a GATED Modal requant of the ORIGINAL bf16 weights to per-32-ue8m0 with an UNCERTAIN PPL outcome (pow2 error 13-41%/scale). NET: the routemap's "+10-12% tok/s via fullk" silently assumed a free pow2 fold — refuted; the mm2d verify is closer to its practical M3 ceiling (54 GB/s, instruction-bound, PROVEN) than assumed, with only a gated+uncertain data-side lever left — and per THIS refutation it too must be validated by intervention, not trusted from the probe alone, before the requant is paid.
