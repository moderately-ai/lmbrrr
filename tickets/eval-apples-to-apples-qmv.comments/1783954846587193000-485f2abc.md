3. WHY are their head kernels faster? Their q4_K inner loop (read in full at ggml-metal.metal:8080, HEAD e920c52) is our common ancestor: identical masks (0x000F/0x0F00/0x00F0/0xF000), identical deferred 1/256+1/16 scales, identical 4-blocks-per-simdgroup y-mapping, same sumy/dmin correction. Structural deltas: (a) GEOMETRY nr0=2 rows/simdgroup x N_SG=2 simdgroups = 64-thread TGs (vs our 4 rows x 1 sg = 32-thread TGs); (b) their y is f32, ours bf16. MLX at 142 GB/s with f16 y (2-byte + convert, like bf16) argues AGAINST y-dtype being the mechanism; geometry/simdgroup-residency is the prime suspect (32-thread TGs may cap resident simdgroups per core and under-hide DRAM latency; note our NSG sweep kept nr0=4 while adding sgs — never tested nr0=2).
