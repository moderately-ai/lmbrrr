## LIVE IMPLEMENTATION + A/B (2026-07-19) — SIMPLE SKIP POLICIES REFUTED

Shipped flags (default OFF):
- `--skip-low-conf <thr>`: after propose, if mean conf < thr → 1-token greedy
- `--skip-after-reject`: after accepted==0 → next step 1-token greedy

### Live results (M3, Q8_0, prose N=128, 3 reps)

| arm | median tps | Δ vs off | skip_rounds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| exact off | 15.34 | — | 0 |

exact skip paths keep **ids byte-match** off → pure slowdown, not quality drift.

### Why offline oracle misled (mechanism)

1. **Oracle inplace (+22%) is non-causal:** it replaces a zero-accept round *after seeing exact_prefix*. Live must decide *before* verify.
2. **Reactive skip-after-reject is the wrong time offset:** it can only cheapen the *next* round. P(next zero|zero)≈0.33 on exact → 67% of reactive skips destroy a productive amortized verify (flat ~218 ms for ~3+ tokens) and replace it with 69 ms for 1 token → net **loss** (−5 to −9%).
3. **Conf thr at recall that catches zeros has bad precision:** thr=1.0 skips 28/38 rounds; many would have accepted. Offline conf EV assumed a **fixed conf/trajectory sequence**; live skips change anchors and future conf → thrashing (more rounds, higher total verify_s).
4. **Bug found+fixed en route:** first build only kept conf under ORACLE_LOG so skip never fired; fixed before the −25% measurement.

### Kill / done-when

Kill criterion was suite ≥ +3%. **FAIL for both simple policies.**  
Flags retained default OFF for future high-precision zero predictors (need prec≫0.5 at useful recall).  
**Do not default on. Do not treat offline skip-on-zero as a free +20%.**
