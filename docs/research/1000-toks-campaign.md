Planes (16B headers | 128B quants, perfect 128B cache-line alignment) vs 144B AoS: +3.7% lm_head, +1-2% projections, bitwise-identical outputs (fork 00adb831, `qmv-soa` gate task). ## 2026-07-15 (synthesis): the acceptance wall is a training-objective/coverage problem, not a data-volume one

Stepping back after r2 (wash) and r3 (near-miss): the mean sits at ~98% of the decode-only greedy floor, held under by writing/summ/qa (80–95%) while math clears 116%+. The binding wall is acceptance on those weak classes — and three data points now say it is **not** a data-volume problem: r1→r2→r3 pushed holdout top-1 monotonically (0.7595 → 0.7759 → 0.7639-quantized) and moved in-loop τ by ~zero. The root cause candidate is the MTP distillation **objective** — teacher-forced single-token cross-entropy optimizes a p1-flavoured proxy, not the accepted-run length τ (which is multiplicative: `E[τ] = 1 + p1 + p1p2 + …`, so p1 is τ's leading factor but holdout top-1 is a single-position, teacher-forced stand-in that decouples from the run under the head's own drifting drafts). q164 is the concrete instance: a τ-collapse depth cannot rescue (a broken leading factor; adding tail terms accepts nothing). **The unexamined gate before any of this:** is regen-r4-400k even *covering* the weak classes? It was built to match the target's token-frequency distribution (fed the FR-Spec ranking), not to be task-balanced — so weak-class acceptance is either a coverage gap (cheap corpus rebalance) or intrinsic entropy (open-ended low-margin generation → partly fundamental; levers shift to relaxed acceptance + tree hedging). A run-aware objective on a corpus that doesn't cover writing won't fix writing, so the corpus/entropy diagnostic runs first and gates the strategy. Protocol promotion: holdout top-1 is a known-weak proxy for τ — bitten twice — and training gains must be confirmed on the suite, never taken from the holdout.

## 2026-07-15 (A0 done): the diagnostic corrects both swings — drafter quality is the limiter, not entropy or the objective alone

Ran the cause diagnostic (target per-class next-token margin + out-of-slice fraction on the deployed q4k target, then causal draft-vocab A/Bs) and it retracts *both* prior over-claims. Not "the objective is the ceiling" (first swing), and not "weak classes are intrinsic-entropy-capped" (the A0 first-pass swing) — a low target margin measures the drafter's *error-tolerance*, not a hard ceiling (a perfect drafter accepts every token regardless of target entropy). The load-bearing evidence: **full-vocab draft gives *lower* τ than the slice** (writing/math τ identical 32k-vs-full; qa/summ τ *lower* at full than 64k). With 100% coverage the drafter should accept more; it accepts less — because more vocab lets the imperfect head argmax to rare wrong tokens. So the **drafter's own quality is the limiter, and training has real headroom** — writing isn't hopeless, it's the *hardest* class (median target margin 1.62, tightest tolerance). The FR-Spec 32k slice is otherwise well-tuned: qa (5.4% oos) and summ (7.8% oos) have a genuine coverage gap (rare content tokens: `acock`, `surrogate`, Indonesian `orang`), 64k recovers τ on those questions — **but global 64k is −1.9% on the suite and fails the bar** (the four 0%-oos classes each pay a ~3% pure draft-compute tax for zero τ gain; τ-vs-vocab is non-monotonic). The real coverage lever is a *targeted/adaptive* slice, not a bigger global N. And the key interaction: acceptance loss = (drafter wrong) + (drafter right but out-of-slice); today "wrong" dominates so coverage is marginal, but **coverage's value grows as the drafter improves** and the residual misses shift to "right-but-out-of-slice." Net: fix the drafter (training/tree — headroom confirmed), keep 32k for now, and the adaptive-slice lever pays off more later.
